# Paid Time Off Policy v2.0 (Superseded)

## Annual Allocation
All regular full-time Kohler employees receive 15 days of paid time off per calendar year accrued at 1.25 days per month. Unused PTO may carry over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following year.

## Leave Request Procedure
Requests for PTO exceeding 3 consecutive days must be submitted to the direct supervisor at least 10 business days in advance.
