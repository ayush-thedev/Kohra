# Severance & Separation Policy

## Standard Severance Formula
In the event of involuntary restructuring without cause, eligible employees receive 2 weeks of base salary per completed year of service, with a minimum guaranteed floor of 4 weeks and a ceiling of 26 weeks. Health insurance continuation (COBRA subsidy) is provided for up to 3 months.

## Executive Separation Guidelines
Executive-level separations (Director and above) require personalized separation agreements authorized by the VP of Human Resources and Legal Counsel. Standard executive packages include 6 to 12 months salary continuation and accelerated vesting subject to board sign-off.
