# Employee Benefits & Wellness Program

## Health & Insurance Coverage
Kohler offers comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance coverage starting on day one of full-time employment. The company subsidizes 80% of employee premiums and 65% of dependent family premiums.

## Retirement & 401(k) Matching
Kohler matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of eligible compensation, plus a 50% match on the subsequent 2% (maximum aggregate company match of 5%).

## Kohler Eco-Stewardship Wellness Benefit
Full-time employees can claim up to $500 annually toward home water conservation systems, Kohler filtration products, or local gym/fitness memberships.
