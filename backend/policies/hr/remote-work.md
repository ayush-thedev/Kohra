# Hybrid & Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid Schedule Guidelines
Eligible corporate employees may work up to 2 days remotely per week with manager approval. Core collaborative in-office days are Tuesdays and Thursdays to facilitate cross-functional alignment.

## Home Office Technology Stipend
A one-time $400 technology allowance is provided upon hire for ergonomics, monitor arms, and certified home equipment. Monthly internet expenses are reimbursable up to $50/month for approved full-remote roles.
