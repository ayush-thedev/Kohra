# Paid Time Off Policy v3.0

## Annual Allocation
Effective January 1, 2026, Kohler enhanced the standard employee annual leave allocation:
- Baseline entitlement: 20 days PTO per year (updated Jan 2026) accrued at 1.67 days per month.
- Employees with 5+ years of tenure receive 25 days PTO per year.
- Up to 8 unused PTO days can be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year.

## Leave Request Procedure
Employees must log leave requests in the Kohler People Hub. Single-day leaves require 24 hours notice; leaves greater than 3 consecutive days require 2 weeks advance notice to the reporting manager.

## Kohler Well-Being & Floating Days
In addition to standard PTO, each employee is granted 2 designated Kohler Wellness Floating Days and 1 Community Water Stewardship volunteer day annually.
