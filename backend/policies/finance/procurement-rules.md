# Corporate Procurement & Purchase Order Guidelines

## Competitive Bidding Rules
Any engagement with third-party suppliers, software vendors, or contractors with an anticipated total annual spend exceeding $50,000 must undergo a formal Request for Proposal (RFP) process with a minimum of 3 competing vendor bids.

## Purchase Order (PO) Issuance
Work cannot commence, nor can software licenses be provisioned, prior to the issuance of an approved Purchase Order generated through the Kohler SAP Ariba procurement system.
