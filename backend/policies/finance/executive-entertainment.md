# Executive Entertainment & Client Hospitality Policy

## Executive Discretionary Hospitality Limits
Executive leadership members (VP level and above) are granted annual discretionary entertainment budgets to host key commercial partners, architects, and enterprise distributors:
- Vice President annual entertainment limit: $50,000 per fiscal year.
- Senior Vice President / Executive Committee: $120,000 per fiscal year.
- Per-event meal ceiling: Up to $250 per attendee for high-level partner dinners.
- Client golf events and hospitality suites at industry design expos: Pre-approved up to $15,000 per event with Controller sign-off.

## Compliance & Documentation
All hospitality expenses exceeding $1,000 must include a documented business attendee roster, corporate affiliation, and strategic rationale submitted directly to the Chief Financial Officer.
