# Travel Per Diem Policy v2.1

## Domestic Travel
- Daily per diem limit: $75/day for meals and incidentals (breakfast $15, lunch $25, dinner $35).
- Pre-approval from direct manager is required for any travel with total estimated costs exceeding $500.
- Itemized receipts are mandatory for any single business expense greater than $25.
- Standard economy airfare must be booked through the Kohler Corporate Travel portal.

## International Travel
- Daily per diem limit: $150/day for international destinations.
- Premium economy or business class is permitted for continuous flights exceeding 8 hours with VP approval.
- Lodging limit: Up to $220/night in standard business hubs; pre-approved up to $300/night in tier-1 high-cost metropolitan centers (e.g., Tokyo, London, NYC).
- Currency conversion calculations are automatically synchronized with Kohler corporate exchange rates on the date of expense.
