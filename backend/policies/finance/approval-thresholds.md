# Financial Approval Thresholds & Delegation of Authority

## Spending Limits by Management Tier
- Team Lead / Direct Manager: Up to $5,000 per transaction within budgeted departmental allocations.
- Senior Manager / Department Head: Up to $25,000 per transaction.
- Vice President: Up to $100,000 per transaction.
- Senior Vice President / C-Suite Officer: Up to $500,000 per transaction.
- CEO & Board Audit Committee: Required for capital expenditures or commitments exceeding $500,000.

## Dual Authorization Requirement
Any single invoice or disbursement greater than $50,000 requires dual authorization comprising the responsible cost center VP and the Corporate Controller.
