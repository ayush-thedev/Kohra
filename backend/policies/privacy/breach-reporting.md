# Privacy Incident & Security Breach Notification Policy

## 72-Hour Regulatory Notification Window
In accordance with GDPR Article 33, CCPA, and global privacy mandates, any confirmed or suspected unauthorized disclosure of sensitive personal data must be escalated to the Kohler Incident Response Team within 2 hours of detection and reported to relevant data protection authorities within seventy-two (72) hours.

## Affected Individual Notifications
Where a security breach poses a high risk to individual consumer rights, direct communication to affected users must be issued without undue delay containing guidance on protective measures, password resets, and credit monitoring where applicable.
