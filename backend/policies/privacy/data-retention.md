# Enterprise Data Retention & Disposal Policy

## Retention Schedules by Classification
- Customer Commercial & Warranty Invoices: Retained for seven (7) years following purchase to satisfy tax and product warranty obligations.
- Smart Home Fixture Telemetry & Diagnostic Logs: Retained for ninety (90) days in active storage, then pseudonymized and aggregated for water analytics before permanent purging after 12 months.
- Employee Personnel & Payroll Files: Retained for the duration of employment plus seven (7) years following separation.
- Candidate Recruitment Profiles: Retained for twenty-four (24) months with opt-in consent.

## Data Sanitization & Cryptographic Erasure
Hard drives and decommissioned cloud storage volumes must undergo DoD 5220.22-M or NIST SP 800-88 compliant cryptographic sanitization prior to physical recycling or decommissioning.
