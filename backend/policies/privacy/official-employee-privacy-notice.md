# Kohler Employee Privacy Notice (Official)

*Source document: `0_official_policies/privacy_policies/Kohler Privacy Notices.pdf` (Kohler Employee Privacy Notice). Applies to Kohler Co. and affiliates/subsidiaries employees located outside the United States, European Union, and United Kingdom, unless a separate notice has been prominently displayed or presented. RBAC restriction to be revisited — currently open to all tiers per interim decision.*

## Scope and Who We Are

The purpose of this Notice is to provide Kohler employees with information about how and why their personal data is processed, their privacy rights, and how the law protects them. It covers: who we are and how to contact us; rights relating to personal data; what data is collected; data from third-party sources; how and why data is used; sharing; transfers; security; storage duration; and automated decisions.

KOHLER Co. is a global company providing products, services, and experiences to consumers, customers, vendors, and suppliers. Kohler is committed to privacy and security of personal information and transparency in processing.

Contact: Kohler Global Data Privacy Team at KohlerGlobalDataPrivacy@Kohler.com (see How to Contact Us). This Notice may be updated from time to time; a revised Notice will be provided or made available.

## Employee Data Categories Collected

All personal data collected from employees directly and from third parties:

- Contact information: work and home address, telephone number, email address, social media handles.
- Identification information: government-issued ID (driver's license, passport), photographs, similar identifiers.
- Immigration status: information verifying employment eligibility.
- Biographical information: name, gender/gender identity, pronouns, date of birth, professional history, language proficiencies, qualifications, references, education, company biography, social media profiles and activity, photo.
- Professional qualifications: designations, licensure, memberships, leadership positions, credentials, continuing education.
- General employment information: department, work location, job title, dates of employment, work status (full-time/part-time), terms and conditions, work history, timekeeping, personnel and disciplinary records, training participation, background checks, drug and alcohol tests and other lawful screens, absence administration, workers' compensation, and emergency services information.
- Compensation, benefits and payroll: salary and bonus, benefits (health insurance, retirement savings), equity awards, bank account details, working time records (vacation, absence, sick leave, leave status, hours worked).
- Performance information: management metrics, evaluations, feedback, promotion history.
- Information about related persons: spouse, domestic/civil partner, dependents, beneficiaries, emergency contacts.
- Credentials, technology, access and system information: company email, usernames, passwords, keycard numbers; use of devices, communications, IT systems and applications (time of use, files accessed, search history, pages viewed, IP address, device ID, geolocation); office and facility access and location (keycard scans, security camera footage).
- Expenses and travel information: business travel and business expenses.
- Healthcare, welfare, and medical information: wellness and employee assistance program participation, executive physicals, health insurance, body temperature, vaccination status, health symptoms and screening/tracking (including travel information, health education) under health and safety plans, including COVID-19 and infectious-disease prevention measures.
- Biometric information: fingerprint scans and voiceprints may be collected in certain Kohler locations.
- Accommodation requests: disabilities or other health conditions needed to evaluate requests.

## Personal Data from Third-Party Sources

In addition to data collected directly, Kohler may collect personal data from:

- Employment agencies or recruiters.
- Job board websites used to apply for a Kohler job.
- Benefits program service providers.
- Prior employers providing employment references.
- Professional references identified on CV/resume or authorized for contact.
- Background check, credit check, or other screening providers (where required and permitted by law).
- Social media profiles or other publicly available sources (publicly-available information).
- Dependents and related persons communicating directly with Kohler.

Additional personal data may be collected during employment-related activities throughout employment.

## How and Why We Use Employee Data

Most common legal bases: Contractual Necessity (perform a contract about to be entered or entered, e.g., employment or share option agreement); Compliance with Law (legal or regulatory obligation); Legitimate Interests (necessary for Kohler's legitimate interests not overridden by individual interests and rights). Special-category data (e.g., health) is processed to carry out employment legal obligations or rights (sickness, sick-pay, workplace accidents), for substantial public interest (equal opportunities monitoring, preventing or detecting unlawful acts), or for establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. See detailed purposes below.

High-level purposes and bases:

- Contractual performance (Contractual Necessity): process and share personal data to perform, administer and manage agreements with the employee.
- Talent management (Legitimate Interests): process and share data for talent management.
- Business operation and improvement (Legitimate Interests): operate and improve products, services, and business generally.
- Systems and premises management (Legitimate Interests): operate, manage and secure IT systems, premises and facilities.
- Compliance and protection (Compliance with Law or Legitimate Interests): compliance and protection purposes including establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
- Corporate transactions (Legitimate Interests): process and disclose data in actual or prospective corporate transactions.
- Privacy protective steps (Legitimate Interests): create aggregated, de-identified and/or anonymised data.
- Further uses: consent is sought where further use is incompatible with the initial purpose; otherwise the original basis applies where compatible.

## Who We Share Employee Data With

- Affiliates: group companies, subsidiaries, and affiliates under the corporate parent — to operate shared infrastructure, systems and technology; group performance reporting; business reorganisation or restructuring; and administration of share plans, pension arrangements or benefits.
- Service providers: payroll administration, benefits and wellness; human resources, occupational health, performance management, training; expense management, travel, transportation and accommodation; IT systems and support, information and physical security; background checks and screenings; equity award administration; corporate banking and credit cards; insurance brokers, claims handlers and loss adjusters, and administrators, nominees, registrars or trustees for benefits plans.
- Employee benefits providers: financial advisors and institutions, pensions providers, insurance providers and intermediaries (e.g., health insurance), and health, fitness, wellness, childcare and concierge providers — to verify eligibility and provide services.
- Professional advisers: accountants, auditors, lawyers, insurers, bankers, and other professional advisors.
- Marketing audience: current and prospective customers and business contacts with whom a Kohler bio is shared on the website or public marketing materials.
- Customers and business partners: companies and individuals with whom Kohler does business or is exploring a relationship.
- Corporate transaction parties: prospective counterparties and advisers in investments, financing, sales, transfers, or mergers; acquirers, successors, or assignees in mergers, acquisitions, asset sales, insolvency, bankruptcy, or receivership. Kohler seeks to minimise amount and sensitivity shared.
- Compliance and protection recipients: regulators, public and judicial bodies with jurisdiction; and parties involved in protecting rights, privacy, safety or property including legal claims.
- Future employers and vendors: where the employee asks for references or the law requires them.
- Other third parties: e.g., mortgage providers, where the employee asks Kohler to disclose data.

## Security Measures

Kohler has put in place security measures designed to prevent personal data from being accidentally lost, used or accessed in an unauthorised way, altered or disclosed.

## Retention of Employee Data

Retention periods are based on business needs and legal requirements. Data is retained as long as necessary for the processing purposes set out in this Notice and any other permissible related purposes, e.g., to comply with regulatory retention duties or litigation holds. When no longer needed, data is either irreversibly anonymised (anonymised data may be retained and used) or securely destroyed.

## No Automated Decisions

Kohler does not envisage employees being subject to decisions or profiling with significant impact based solely on automated decision-making.

## Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

Under certain circumstances, by law employees may have the right to:

- Request access to personal data (copy and lawfulness check).
- Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data.
- Request erasure where there is no good reason to continue processing, including after objecting.
- Object to processing based on Legitimate Interests due to particular personal circumstances.
- Request restriction (suspension) of processing, e.g., to establish accuracy or reason for processing.
- Request transfer (data portability) of provided data in structured, commonly used, machine-readable format to the employee or a chosen third party — applies to automated processing based on consent or contract performance.

How to exercise: contact Kohler using the details in How to Contact Us. Identity verification and follow-up information may be requested as a security measure. Some rights may not apply or some data categories may be exempt; Kohler will notify the employee where so. Kohler tries to respond to legitimate requests within one month, longer for complex or multiple requests with notification. Complaints may be made via the same contact details; Kohler replies as soon as possible.
