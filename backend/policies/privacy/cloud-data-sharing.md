# Cloud Vendor Data Sharing & Transfer Policy

## Mandatory Data Protection Addendum (DPA)
Under no circumstances may customer personally identifiable information (PII), employee data, or Kohler proprietary telemetry be shared with or hosted by any third-party cloud service provider without an executed Kohler Data Protection Addendum (DPA). The DPA must mandate:
- Strict purpose limitation (processing data exclusively on Kohler's documented instructions).
- AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit.
- Immediate 24-hour breach notification obligation upon the vendor.
- Guaranteed right to audit vendor security postures and SOC 2 Type II compliance reports.

## Cross-Border International Data Transfers
Transfers of European or UK personal data to cloud infrastructure located in non-adequate jurisdictions require incorporation of standard contractual clauses (SCCs) and a mandatory Data Transfer Impact Assessment (DTIA) approved by the Kohler Chief Privacy Officer.
