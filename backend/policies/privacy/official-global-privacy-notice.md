# Kohler Global Privacy Notice (Official)

*Last Updated: December 10, 2025. Source document: Kohler Global Privacy Policy (kohler_global_privacy_policy.md in 0_official_policies/privacy_policies/).*

KOHLER Co. is a global company on a mission to help people live gracious, healthy and sustainable lives. This Privacy Notice describes how KOHLER Co. and its affiliates and subsidiaries (together, "Kohler", "we", "us", "our") process personal information in connection with the activities described in the Scope section below.

For questions about this Notice, contact the Kohler Global Data Privacy Team (see How to Contact Us).

## Scope and Applicability

This Privacy Notice applies to processing of personal information collected:

- Via our websites, mobile applications, social media pages, and other digital properties that display or link to this Privacy Notice.
- From our customers and prospective customers, including former, current and prospective customers and others with whom we currently do or have done business.
- From business partners, vendors and service providers, including former, current, and prospective business partners, vendors and service providers with whom we do or have done business.

This Privacy Notice does not apply to:

- Personal information we collect from our employees, contractors, or job applicants (see Employee Privacy Notice).
- Personal information collected in connection with Kohler's hospitality business ("Kohler, Wisconsin") — see Kohler, Wisconsin Privacy Policy.
- Personal information collected in connection with Kohler's health business ("Kohler Health") — see Kohler Health Privacy Policy.
- Any personal information Kohler processes on behalf of a third-party entity, such as a business customer or vendor.

Jurisdiction-specific supplements apply: United States State Privacy Rights Notice, and Supplemental Notices for EEA and UK, Brazil, Canada, and China.

## Information We Collect — Provided by You

- Personal or business contact information: name, billing and mailing address, telephone number, professional title and company name, email address.
- Transaction and payment information: banking or credit card details, transaction details (billing and delivery address, order number), and other information needed to complete transactions.
- User-generated content: comments, questions, messages, images, videos, and other content submitted online or offline, plus associated metadata.
- Communications information: information provided when interacting with Customer Service (including call recordings), direct email or mail contact, and chat features including chatbots.
- Demographic information: city, state, country of residence, postal code, and age.
- Government-issued identification: national ID (e.g., Social Security Number, tax ID, passport number), state or local ID (e.g., driver's license), and images of the ID card.
- Marketing information: preferences for receiving marketing communications and engagement details.
- Account information: username and password for online accounts, plus profile information.
- Promotion information: information shared when entering competitions, promotions, or surveys, including contact data for winner notification, eligibility verification, and prize delivery.
- Other information not specifically listed, used as described in this Notice or as disclosed at collection.

## Information Collected Automatically

- App, browser, and device information: device, operating system, browser, IP address, plug-ins, and network characteristics.
- Usage and activity information: pages viewed, clicks, service usage, diagnostics, crash data, performance logs, and error reports.
- Session replay information: interaction with sites and webforms (IP address, session time and details), including TrustedForm session replay technology.
- Communication interaction information: interactions with email or other communications, collected via pixel tags (clear GIFs) embedded in emails.
- Cookies and similar technologies: see Kohler Cookie Policy. Includes cookies and code to operate chat and artificial intelligence (AI) technologies and related features, which may access webpages visited, IP address, and personal information shared through chats or inputs.

## Information from Affiliates and Third Parties

- Kohler group companies (Affiliates): personal contact, financial, and usage information obtained as a normal part of conducting business.
- Marketing and advertising partners and event co-sponsors: name, contact information, marketing content viewed, and site interactions.
- Analytics providers: information about site interactions.
- Public sources: social media platforms, government agencies, public records, and other publicly available sources.
- Entities to which we provide products and services: retailers, distributors, and franchisees.
- Referral sources: entities that provide referrals.
- Business transaction partners: entities in mergers, acquisitions, asset sales, or similar transactions, and in insolvency, bankruptcy, or receivership.

## How Kohler Uses Personal Information

Delivery, personalization, and improvement of products and services:

- Provide products and services; create and maintain Kohler accounts; provide customer support; send product- or service-related communications; fulfill event registrations; send administrative information (guarantees, warranties, terms changes); customize experience including device-based settings; conduct research and development and improve products including trend analysis; machine learning in connection with products, customer service, and R&D, with AI-model training on personal information only where the user has opted in.

Marketing and advertising:

- Send and measure direct marketing about products and news of likely interest; facilitate interest-based advertising, including via cookies and automatic collection described above.

Compliance and protection:

- Comply with laws, lawful requests, and legal process (subpoenas, investigations, government requests); protect rights, privacy, safety and property (including legal claims); audit internal processes for legal, contractual, and policy compliance; enforce governing terms and conditions; prevent, identify, investigate and deter fraudulent, harmful, unauthorized, unethical or illegal activity, including cyberattacks and identity theft.

Additional uses:

- Create aggregated, de-identified and/or anonymized data by removing identifying information; never attempt reidentification; use and share such data for lawful business purposes including analytics, promotion, and AI-model and machine learning training.
- Share personal information in actual or prospective corporate events (see Sharing section).
- Further uses incompatible with the initial collection purpose require consent.

## Sharing Your Information

Kohler shares personal information with Affiliates, service providers, and other third parties to provide services:

- Advertising partners: third-party advertising companies for interest-based advertising.
- Affiliates: transfers between Affiliates as a normal part of business and service offering.
- Authorities and others: law enforcement, government authorities, and private parties as believed necessary in good faith to comply with laws, protect rights and safety, and related compliance purposes.
- Business transferees: acquirers, successors, or assignees in actual or prospective transactions, insolvency, bankruptcy, or receivership where personal information is a business asset.
- Other users and the public: content posted on message boards, chat, profile pages, blogs, social media, or messages sent through our systems may be visible to other users and the public.
- Partners: co-sponsors of events or promotions, joint product or service providers, or parties whose products may be of interest.
- Professional advisors: lawyers, auditors, bankers, and insurers in the course of professional services.
- Third-party service providers: website hosting, consulting and monitoring, chatbot and AI technology providers, data analysis, IT infrastructure, customer service, email delivery, auditing, payment processing, and other services.
- Third parties designated by you: where you instruct us or provide consent.

## Retention of Your Information

We retain information as required to fulfil collection purposes, including providing products and services, complying with legal obligations, and protecting our or others' interests. Retention periods vary by country; internal policies consider:

- Length of ongoing relationship and service provision (e.g., while an account is active or products are used).
- Length of client relationship and service provision.
- Legal obligations (e.g., laws requiring transaction or communication records be kept for a set period).
- Legal position considerations (statutes of limitations, litigation, regulatory investigations).

When personal information is no longer required, we delete or anonymize it. Where deletion is not possible (e.g., backup archives), we securely store and isolate it from further processing until deletion is possible. Anonymized information (no longer associable with the individual) may be used indefinitely without further notice.

## Data Security and Integrity

We have implemented organizational, technical, and physical security measures designed to protect personal information. Despite these efforts, no data security measures can guarantee absolute security. Users can help keep data safe by taking reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized disclosure or misuse.

## Your Controls and Choices

Rights and choices available to all users (US state and EEA/UK residents: see jurisdiction-specific supplements):

- Access or update your information: registered account holders may review and update certain account information by logging in or using the Account Actions methods below.
- Opt-out of communications: opt out of marketing emails via unsubscribe instructions or by contacting us; service-related and non-marketing emails may continue. Text messages: reply "STOP" to opt out.
- Cookies and automatic technologies: see Kohler Cookie Policy for controls.
- Do Not Track: we currently do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals. Learn more at allaboutdnt.com.
- Global Privacy Control (GPC): where we detect a GPC opt-out signal for data "sales" as defined under certain laws, we make reasonable efforts to respect those choices as required by applicable law.
- Declining to provide information: required or mandatory information is needed to provide certain services; without it we may be unable to provide those services.
- Linked third-party platforms: connecting via social media or third-party platforms is governed by that platform's settings; revoking access does not apply retroactively to already-received information.
- Account Actions Information: submit information/know, access, appeal, correction, or deletion requests at https://privacy.kohler.com/, toll free at 1-800-923-1138, or via email to KohlerGlobalDataPrivacy@kohler.com.

## Third-Party Services

This Notice does not address, and Kohler is not responsible for, the privacy, information security, or other practices of third parties, including any third-party website, application, or service linked from our services. Use of third-party services is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

## Children's Personal Information

Sites and services are not directed to individuals under age sixteen (16), and Kohler does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 16. Do not provide personal information through sites or services if under 16. If a user is suspected of being younger than 16, Kohler will take steps to delete the information as soon as possible.

## Changes to Our Privacy Notice

We may update this notice to reflect changes, indicating changes by posting the new version and effective date on the website or by other appropriate means. Periodically review the website and Notice. Use of services after the effective date of a modified Notice indicates acknowledgement that the modified Notice applies.

## How to Contact Us

Questions, complaints, or concerns regarding this Privacy Notice: email KohlerGlobalDataPrivacy@kohler.com, call toll free at 1-800-923-1138, or write to 444 Highland Drive MS 005, Kohler, WI 53044, Attn: Legal Department.
