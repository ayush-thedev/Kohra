# Kohler India Website Privacy Policy (Official)

*Source document: `0_official_policies/privacy_policies/Safeguarding Privacy & Data Protection _ Kohler India.pdf` (KohlerIndiaPrivacyPolicy). Operator: Kohler India Corporation Private Limited, registered office at 26A, Lajpat Nagar, New Delhi, Delhi 110024. Website: https://www.kohler.co.in/.*

In this policy, "user" refers to persons registered on the Kohler India website, including users viewing and users purchasing. The site is a B2C intermediary platform for sanitary ware and kitchen and bath products. Orders, payments, and interactions are governed by the site Terms and Conditions, which the user agrees to be bound by. This Policy supplements and is incorporated into the Agreement; in case of conflict, this Policy prevails.

## Scope and Operator

Kohler India is committed to safeguarding privacy of website visitors and users. The web server uses extended log format capturing: date and time of visit, referring address, browser type, and visitor IP address. The site uses cookies to improve online customer experience; by using the website, the user consents to cookie use per this policy.

## Personal Data We Collect

Kohler strives to collect only needed personal data. Categories collected, stored and used:

- Computer and visit data: IP address, geographical location, browser type and version, operating system, referral source, length of visit, page views, navigation, and data from Google Analytics or other analytical tools.
- Data entered into the website by the user.
- Registration data: personal information, company details, GSTIN, email ID, address, contact information.
- Data via social media properties.
- Data via HTML-formatted email messages linking to this Policy.
- Data via registration for newsletters, seminars, webinars and events.
- Any other information the user chooses to send.
- Stored user preferences for convenience and personalization.
- Biometric Data: physical or human characteristics identifying a person (voiceprint, fingerprints, facial shape, finger scan). May be collected, used, or handled for connected products (smart products, IoT devices). Biometric information in any captured, converted, stored, or shared form based on a biometric identifier is processed in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and Rules as amended.
- Social/third-party login data: registering or logging in via Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, Yahoo, Google, etc. may collect Personally Identifiable Information (name, age, gender, postal address, telephone, email, company name, mobile/fixed line numbers, PAN, service tax registration, background information, web-activity) subject to third-party permissions. Additional information may be required to register, subscribe, or improve experience.
- Cancellation: users may request cancellation or removal of registered information by emailing cust_service@kohler.com (permission may or may not be granted). After confirmation and response, the Company in most cases removes information from its "live" database; residual data may remain in archives. Accounts terminated for Agreement violations may retain registration data to prevent re-registration.

## Cookies and Tracking

This policy describes cookie types and operation. By using the Site, users expressly consent to Kohler India Corporation Private Limited using cookies on their device, with the option to opt out of or block certain cookies.

- A cookie is a file with an identifier (letters and numbers) sent by a web server to a browser, stored by the browser, and sent back on each page request, enabling identification and tracking.
- Cookies make the Site work properly, provide relevant products and services, enhance experience, and help understand usage (e.g., popular pages).
- Categories: strictly necessary cookies (required, cannot be disabled, no marketing data); performance cookies (anonymous visitor usage, pages visited, errors); functionality cookies (remember choices such as username, language, region; enhanced or customized features; may be anonymized; cannot track browsing on other sites).
- Duration types: session cookies (stored only during Site use, deleted on browser close; used to track navigation, selected services portfolio, prevent fraud, increase security); persistent cookies (stored for a specific lifespan varying per cookie; used to recognize returning users and remember preferences).
- Third-party website cookies: being directed to third-party service sites may set their own cookies beyond Kohler's control.
- Cookie questions: contact KohlerGlobalDataPrivacy@kohler.com.

Personal information submitted via the website is used for purposes specified here or in relevant site sections. Personal information includes name, address, mobile number, email ID, and other contact details shared at registration.

Purposes:

- Administer the website.
- Improve browsing experience by personalizing the website.
- Enable use of services available on the website.
- Send marketing/commercial communications consented to via the terms and conditions.
- Send specifically requested email notifications.
- Deal with enquiries and complaints made by or about the user relating to the website.
- Keep the website secure and prevent fraud.
- Send transaction-related information.
- Publish user-submitted publication content per the license granted by the user.
- Provide personal information to third parties for direct/indirect marketing where the user has expressly consented by accepting this Policy.
- Disclose personal information where mandatory under Indian laws or court orders/judgments of competent jurisdiction.

## Sharing and Transferring Data

- Disclosure to employees, officers, authorised dealers, agents or sub-contractors as reasonably necessary for purposes in this policy.
- Further disclosure: as required by law; in connection with legal proceedings or prospective proceedings; to establish, exercise or defend legal rights (including fraud prevention and credit-risk reduction); to persons reasonably believed likely to obtain a court or authority disclosure order.
- Except as provided here, user information is not provided to third parties. The user expressly agrees to such transfers.
- Third-party specialists: Kohler employs third-party companies and persons for specific tasks (mail courier, email delivery, money transactions, etc.) and may share personally identifiable information as needed to provide subscribed products or services. Agents have no right to use shared data beyond what is necessary to perform tasks efficiently, unless otherwise informed.

## How We Protect Your Data

- Reasonable technical and organizational precautions against loss, misuse, or alteration; storage on secure (encrypted, password- and firewall-protected) servers.
- Users are responsible for keeping passwords and user details confidential. Kohler never asks for passwords (except at login) and never sends emails requesting online payments; transactions responding to phishing emails are at the user's own risk with no Kohler liability.
- Best-level industry-standard cyber security; periodic privacy audits; compliance with Reasonable Security Practices and Rules under the Information Technology Act, 2000.
- Industry-standard database protections (e.g., firewalls, access limits where appropriate). No perfect Internet security exists.
- Misuse reporting: improper collection or misuse of site information violates the Terms of Use and should be reported to support@kohlerindia.com. User-posted Content is at the user's own risk; removal does not guarantee deletion from caches, archives, or copies made by other users.
- Users must keep provided personal information updated and make corrections promptly.

## Your Controls and Choices

- Users may request any personal information held about them, subject to identity evidence (usually a solicitor- or bank-certified passport photocopy plus an original utility bill showing current address).

## Retention of Data

- Data is kept as long as the user account is active or per internal policies. Longer retention applies where directed by courts or legal authorities.

## India Privacy Rights

- Right to access notice and consent in native language: read this policy or any personal-information request in clear plain language, with the option to access it in English or any Eighth Schedule constitutional language.
- Right to access information about personal data: summary of all processing activities; identities of all persons with whom data has been shared plus description of data shared.
- Right to correction and erasure: correction, completion, updating and erasure of consented data per applicable law — correct inaccurate or misleading data; complete incomplete data; update data; erase data upon request unless retention is necessary for the specified purpose or legal compliance.
- Right of grievance redressal: readily available grievance redressal means provided by Kohler.
- Right to nominate: nominate another individual to exercise privacy rights upon death or incapacity.
- Right to withdraw consent: withdraw earlier consent at any point.

## Minors and Persons with Disability

Goods and services are not directed to individuals under age Eighteen (18); Kohler does not knowingly collect Personal Information from individuals under 18. Kohler does not knowingly offer goods and services to persons incapacitated to contract under the Indian Contract Act due to disability.

## Cross-Border Transfer

Transfer of personal data to another jurisdiction is allowed only if that country ensures the same level of protection, and only if necessary for performance of a lawful contract between the body corporate (or its agent) and the information provider, or where the provider has consented.

## Breach Intimation

In a personal data breach, Kohler provides necessary intimation to the Data Protection Board and to the affected user. Policy-change notifications are deemed incorporated from the notification date.

## Contact and Policy Changes

- Clarifications: info@kohlerindia.com.
- The Company may amend this Policy from time to time. Current collection use is subject to the Policy in effect at the time of use. Users should routinely check for updates; amendments bind users from website/portal update time.
