# Data Subject Access & Privacy Rights Policy

## Data Subject Request (DSAR) Handling SLA
Customers and employees possess statutory rights to request copies of their personal data, rectify inaccuracies, or demand deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"). All verified requests must be processed and resolved within thirty (30) calendar days through the Kohler Privacy Portal.

## Identity Verification Protocols
Before releasing or deleting any personal records, identity verification is conducted via two-factor confirmation or verified government ID matching to prevent unauthorized exfiltration.
