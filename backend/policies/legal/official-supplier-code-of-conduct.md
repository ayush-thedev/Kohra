# Kohler Supplier Code of Conduct (Official)

*Source document: `0_official_policies/legal_and_compliance/Kohler-Supplier-Code-of-Conduct_English.pdf` (Form K-3234, Revision 12, Effective October 14, 2024). Published by Kohler Global Procurement.*

## Introduction and Scope

Kohler and its affiliates conduct business in accordance with the highest ethical standards and in compliance with UN Guiding Principles and the laws of countries where products are produced, bought, and sold. Suppliers are responsible for monitoring adherence to this Code for themselves and their supply chains (any third parties they do business with, including subcontractors). All suppliers must demonstrate compliance at Kohler's request.

Where local laws differ from this Code, suppliers must comply with the more stringent standard. Kohler reserves the right to request and review supporting documents to verify compliance. Kohler will only request documents related to the engagement with Kohler and will respect supplier confidential information and intellectual property rights. If a supplier becomes aware of a breach or reasonable risk of violation, the supplier must notify Kohler as soon as practicable.

Suppliers must take all necessary corrective actions and prepare Corrective Action Preventative Action (CAPA) plans. Kohler reserves the right to terminate business at any time for non-compliance. The Code may be reviewed and updated as needed; suppliers should regularly consult https://www.kohlercompany.com/suppliers/conducting-business/.

## Laws and Regulations

Suppliers must operate in full compliance with all applicable laws and regulations of the countries in which they operate. If compliance with this Code breaches applicable law, the applicable law shall control.

## Supply Chain Compliance

Any third party (including subcontractors) participating in manufacture of Kohler products or components must be compliant with this Code or a substantially equivalent alternative. Kohler reserves the right to request proof of supply chain compliance.

## Data Protection

Suppliers must keep Kohler employees' personal information confidential and secure. Suppliers must comply with all local and international laws and directives for collection, protection, transfer, cross-border transfer, access, limitation, and storage of personal information, including GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

## Intellectual Property

Suppliers must adequately store Kohler's intellectual property rights in a manner that safeguards against misuse, mishandling, counterfeiting, fraud, or improper disclosure, in accordance with applicable local and international law.

## Human Trafficking, Slavery, and Forced Labor

All forms of human trafficking, slavery, forced, bonded (including debt bondage), forced prison labor, or indentured labor of any kind are forbidden, including physical abuse and corporal punishment. This includes transporting, harboring, recruiting, transferring, or receiving persons by means of threat, force, coercion, abduction, or fraud for labor or services. Suppliers must comply with the California Transparency in Supply Chains Act of 2010, UK Modern Slavery Act, and other applicable laws.

- No unreasonable restrictions on workers' freedom of movement in the facility or upon entering or exiting company-provided facilities.
- Workers must be provided with all employment documents in a language they understand, with terms and conditions of employment, before departing their country of origin.
- No substitution or changes in documentation upon arrival unless to meet local law with equal or better terms.
- All work must be voluntary; workers must be free to terminate employment.
- Employers and agents may not hold, destroy, conceal, confiscate, or deny access to identity or immigration documents unless required by law.
- Factory workers must not be required to pay recruitment fees or related fees for hiring or continued employment.

## Child Labor

All workers must be at least the local legal age, above the oldest compulsory school age, and no less than 15 years old. Suppliers must comply with all local legal requirements for work of authorized young workers, particularly hours, wages, and working conditions.

## Health and Safety

Suppliers must provide a safe and healthy working environment in accordance with applicable laws. Workers must have access to first-aid, fire exits, safety equipment, potable water, clean toilet facilities, and eating facilities. If residential facilities are provided, they must be clean and safe with adequate personal space, entry and exit privileges, emergency egresses, heat and ventilation, and water for bathing and showering.

## Work Hours

Except in extraordinary circumstances, workers must not work more than the lesser of sixty (60) hours per week including overtime or the limits allowed by applicable law. All workers must be entitled to at least one day off in every consecutive seven-day period.

## Wages and Benefits

Wages must be set at or above local minimum wage and compensation requirements including overtime, as defined by applicable laws, agreements, and local regulations for regular work, overtime work, production rates, and other compensation elements and employee benefits.

## Non-Discrimination

Employment (hiring, salary, benefits, promotion, training, discipline, termination, retirement, or any other terms and conditions) must be based on merit and ability to perform job requirements, not on race, creed, color, religion, sex, age, national origin, disabilities, veteran status, marital status, sexual orientation, gender, gender identity or expression, military status, or any other protected characteristic.

## Whistleblower Protection

Suppliers must ensure whistleblower confidentiality protection and prohibit retaliation against workers who come forward in good faith and/or refuse an order in violation of this Code. Workers must have an anonymous complaint mechanism to report workplace grievances and Code violations in accordance with local laws.

## Freedom of Association and Collective Bargaining

Suppliers must recognize and respect workers' rights to exercise lawful rights of free association, including joining or not joining any association. Suppliers must respect the legal right of workers to bargain collectively.

## Harassment or Abuse

Every worker must be treated with respect and dignity. No worker must be subject to any physical, sexual, psychological, or verbal harassment or abuse. Suppliers must not use monetary fines as a disciplinary practice.

## Communication

Suppliers must communicate to all workers and supervisors, in a language understandable to them, the protections and benefits afforded by applicable law and this Code.

## Monitoring and Compliance

Kohler may undertake announced and unannounced on-site audits of production factories to monitor compliance. Suppliers must maintain all documentation necessary to demonstrate compliance on-site, and allow Kohler representatives full access to production facilities, worker records, production records, and workers for confidential interviews. Suppliers must not improperly impact audit results including falsified records or coaching of workers.

## Environment and Sustainability

Suppliers must comply with all applicable environmental laws and regulations. Suppliers must strive for continuous improvement in operations and procurement to reduce environmental impacts throughout the product life cycle (carbon and water footprint, resource utilization). Suppliers must provide any requested data to Kohler within 30 days of request, including full material disclosures, production resource information, final points of assembly, and transportation modes and distances.

## Restricted Materials List

Suppliers must adhere to Kohler's Product Environmental Policy - Restricted Materials List and all relevant applicable laws. No products or materials may be designed, developed, manufactured, sold, purchased, or distributed without adherence to applicable product composition regulations.

## Responsible Sourcing of Minerals

Suppliers must comply with applicable laws regarding conflict minerals (tin, tungsten, tantalum, gold, cobalt). Conflict minerals must not directly or indirectly perpetuate human rights abuses. Suppliers must exercise due diligence on source and chain of custody, maintain records, and provide Kohler with verification of source upon request. Suppliers must require the same from their supply chain.

## Gifts, Meals, Entertainment, and Hospitality

Gifts, meals, entertainment, hospitality, trips, or anything of value lacking a legitimate business purpose may be viewed as bribes or kickbacks. It is Kohler policy that gifts or gratuities must not be offered or sent to any Kohler associate. Generally distributed promotional items with corporate logos are allowed on an infrequent basis.

## Conflicts of Interest

Suppliers must not enter into any transaction with Kohler employees that could create an actual or perceived conflict of interest. A conflict of interest is any situation where an individual's interests or relationships could inappropriately influence decisions made on Kohler's behalf. Any potential conflict must be disclosed.

## Anti-Bribery

Suppliers must conduct business with honesty and integrity and demonstrate the highest standards of ethical behavior. Suppliers must not make or receive any corrupt or inappropriate payment on behalf of itself, any third-party, or Kohler and must comply with all applicable anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws including the US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act, UK Bribery Act, and other applicable international and local laws. Suppliers must exercise adequate care when selecting third parties and paying fair market value for services rendered.
