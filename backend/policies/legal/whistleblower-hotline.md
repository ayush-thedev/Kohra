# Whistleblower Hotline & Ethics Reporting Policy

## Confidential Reporting Channels
Kohler provides a 24/7/365 multilingual, confidential, and anonymous compliance hotline operated by an independent third party (EthicsPoint). Reports can be submitted via web portal or toll-free telephone.

## Absolute Non-Retaliation Guarantee
Kohler strictly prohibits any form of retaliation, demotion, termination, or harassment against any employee who reports suspected financial fraud, safety violations, harassment, or regulatory breaches in good faith. Any manager engaging in retaliatory acts faces immediate termination and potential civil liability.
