# Global Regulatory Compliance & Water Stewardship Standards

## EPA WaterSense & Environmental Compliance
All Kohler plumbing fixture designs sold in North America must meet or exceed EPA WaterSense specifications, reducing water consumption by at least 20% compared to federal baseline standards without compromising performance.

## Global Trade Sanctions & Export Controls
All cross-border commercial shipments and software access must be screened against OFAC, EU, and UN denied-parties lists prior to delivery or licensing authorization.
