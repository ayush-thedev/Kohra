# Third-Party Vendor Contracting & Risk Management Policy

## Master Services Agreement (MSA) Legal Terms
All engagements involving third-party suppliers, SaaS platforms, or external consultants require a formal Kohler Master Services Agreement (MSA) or legally vetted Statement of Work (SOW). Key non-negotiable clauses include:
- Uncapped indemnification for vendor intellectual property infringement, data security breaches, and willful misconduct.
- Limitation of Kohler liability capped at aggregate fees paid in the preceding 12 months.
- Mandatory compliance with Kohler Supplier Code of Conduct and Environmental Stewardship Standards.

## Data Processing & Cloud Vendor Prerequisites
Before engaging any vendor that will store, process, or transmit company data or customer records, the business owner must obtain dual sign-offs:
1. Legal approval of the Master Services Agreement incorporating mandatory confidentiality and data indemnity terms.
2. Privacy team approval verifying an executed Data Protection Addendum (DPA) and security risk evaluation.
