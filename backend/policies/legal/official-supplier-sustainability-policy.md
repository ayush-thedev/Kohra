# Kohler Supplier Sustainability Policy (Official)

*Source: `0_official_policies/legal_and_compliance/WF1180400_2025_OPS_SupplierSustainabilityPolicy-English.pdf` (Rev 3, Effective January 1, 2025). Published by Kohler Global Procurement Operations.*

## Purpose and Scope

Kohler and its global subsidiaries and affiliates are committed to ensuring all purchased goods and services are produced under conditions reflecting high standards of social and environmental responsibility. This Supplier Sustainability Policy (SSP) applies to all Kohler suppliers and establishes minimum sustainability expectations. Where local laws differ, suppliers must comply with the more stringent standard. Non-compliance can be grounds for termination. Kohler reserves the right to make any assessment or audit it deems appropriate.

## Policy Areas

Seven key areas across the full supply chain:

1. Business Integrity and Ethics
2. Legal Compliance
3. Health and Safety
4. Human Rights and Labor Standards
5. Environmental Protection
6. Sustainable Material and Product Compliance
7. Social Responsibility

## Business Integrity and Ethics

Suppliers must prevent corruption, extortion, embezzlement, fraud, and anti-competitive behavior. Policies must be in place for integrity of employees and business partners. Adequate records and accounts must accurately reflect business performance and allow audits.

## Legal Compliance

Suppliers must comply with all applicable laws and regulations of the countries in which they operate.

## Health and Safety

Suppliers must provide a safe and healthy working environment, including:

- Proactively identifying and addressing hazards through regular assessment and mitigation programs.
- Providing health and safety training to all employees.
- Maintaining incident reporting and investigation systems with corrective actions.
- Documenting, monitoring, investigating, and reporting workplace incidents.

## Human Rights and Labor Standards

- No forced, bonded, trafficked, or indentured labor; workers must be free to leave employment with reasonable notice without penalty.
- Minimum employment age must be the greater of 15 years, the local legal minimum age, or the age above compulsory schooling. Young workers must not work more than 8 hours per day. No workers under 18 may perform hazardous work.
- Working hours must comply with local laws and not exceed 60 hours per week including overtime. At least one day off per seven-day period. Overtime must be voluntary and compensated at legally mandated rates.
- Wages must meet or exceed local legal minimums. Benefits must comply with local law. No unauthorized deductions from wages as disciplinary measure.
- No discrimination based on race, color, age, gender, sexual orientation, ethnic or social origin, disability, pregnancy, political opinion, religion, trade union membership, marital status, or any other protected status.
- Suppliers must recognize the right of employees to freedom of association and collective bargaining in accordance with local laws.

## Environmental Protection

- Suppliers must have an environmental policy and strive to reduce emissions, effluents, waste, and resource consumption.
- Suppliers must have procedures to measure, monitor, and continuously improve environmental performance.
- Must comply with all applicable environmental laws and regulations including environmental permits and reporting requirements.
- Must minimize greenhouse gas emissions, manage waste responsibly, and reduce water consumption.
- Must have a hazardous substances management program in place.

## Sustainable Material and Product Compliance

- Suppliers must comply with all applicable laws on substance restrictions including REACH, RoHS, and other relevant regulations.
- Kohler's Product Environmental Policy Restricted Materials List applies; suppliers must ensure compliance.
- Conflict minerals (tin, tungsten, tantalum, gold, cobalt) must be sourced responsibly with due diligence on the source and chain of custody.
- Suppliers must provide full material disclosures and production resource information to Kohler upon request within 30 days.

## Social Responsibility

- Suppliers must ensure that their company acts as a responsible member of the community.
- Suppliers must ensure that their activities do not negatively impact the communities in which they operate.

## Third Party Obligations

Suppliers must ensure that their supply chain and subcontractors are aware of and compliant with this policy. Kohler reserves the right to request proof of supply chain compliance.

## Assessment and Monitoring

Kohler may assess compliance through self-assessment questionnaires, desktop reviews, and on-site audits (announced or unannounced). Suppliers must maintain documentation demonstrating compliance, provide access to facilities, worker records, and production records, and allow confidential worker interviews. Suppliers must not improperly impact audit results.

## Non-Compliance and Corrective Action

Where non-compliance is identified, Kohler will work with the supplier to establish an improvement plan and verify progress. Failure to address non-compliance may result in termination of the business relationship. Kohler reserves the right to terminate immediately for severe non-compliance.
