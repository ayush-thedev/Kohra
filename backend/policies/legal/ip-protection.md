# Intellectual Property & Design Rights Protection Policy

## Patent & Industrial Design Filings
All proprietary Kohler innovations including industrial designs, hydrotherapy nozzle patterns, smart sensor algorithms, and aesthetic faucet silhouettes are subject to global patent and trademark filings managed by the Corporate IP Counsel.

## Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)
Before disclosing confidential engineering prototypes, casting techniques, or unreleased product roadmaps to external fabricators or design partners, a bilateral Kohler Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (MNDA) must be fully executed.
