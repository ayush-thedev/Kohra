# Kohler 2024 Global Impact Report (Official)

*Source: `0_official_policies/legal_and_compliance/Kohler_Co_2024_Global-Impact-Report.pdf` (57 pages). Covers Jan 1 to Dec 31, 2024. Excludes Kohler Energy (separated as Rehlko). Prepared with reference to GRI Standards.*

## About Kohler Co.

Founded in 1873, one of the oldest and largest privately held U.S. companies. Global leader in kitchen and bath products, luxury cabinetry/tile/lighting, wellness, hospitality, and golf. Aligned with UN SDGs: Clean Water and Sanitation, Affordable and Clean Energy, Responsible Consumption and Production. 29,000+ associates in 32 countries. HQ: Kohler, Wisconsin.

## Report Scope

Data from Jan 1 to Dec 31, 2024, with three years of context where available. Boundary excludes Kohler Energy (Rehlko, majority owned by Platinum Equity). Newly acquired entities integrated within two years. GRI Content Index, data tables, assurance statement, and partnership info linked in Appendix Overview.

## Reducing Environmental Impact

- Beijing Faucets plant: first China location to earn zero-waste factory recognition.
- Casa Grande, Arizona facility: smart factory with sustainability metrics for energy efficiency, GHG minimization, water use reduction, solid waste reduction.
- Sustainability Champions program: employees driving environmental initiatives.

### GHG Emissions

Scope 1 (direct) and Scope 2 (purchased energy) tracked and reported. Reduction strategies: energy efficiency, fuel switching, renewable energy procurement. Scope 3 addressed across value chain: purchased goods, transportation, use of sold products, end-of-life treatment. Supplier engagement and product design are key levers.

### Supplier Sustainability

Suppliers must meet the Supplier Sustainability Policy covering environmental performance, labor standards, health and safety, and business ethics. Assessments include self-assessment questionnaires and on-site audits.

### Renewable Energy

Increasing renewable energy through power purchase agreements, on-site solar, and renewable energy certificates.

### Waste Management

Waste reduction, recycling, and responsible disposal. Goals: divert waste from landfill, increase recycled content.

### Product Transparency

Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs), Health Product Declarations (HPDs), and other transparency documents provided for customers.

### Innovation for Good

Products addressing water scarcity, energy consumption, and accessibility: water-efficient fixtures, smart home technologies, wellness products.

## Water Stewardship

- Uncompromising Design: products reduce water consumption without compromising performance. WaterSense-labeled products exceed EPA standards by at least 20%.
- Watershed Restoration: investments in watershed protection and replenishment.
- Safe Water for All: initiatives to provide safe water access in underserved communities.
- Operational Water Management: tracking and managing water use, setting reduction targets, implementing recycling and reuse.
- Water-Efficient Products: low-flow faucets, dual-flush toilets, water-saving showerheads.

## Investing in Our Communities

- Focus areas: arts and education, community development, health and human services.
- Partnerships: John Michael Kohler Arts Center, Arts/Industry residency (50th anniversary in 2024), community development organizations.
- Stewardship Giving: financial and in-kind support to aligned nonprofits.
- Believing in Better: creating lasting positive change through arts, education, and community development.

## Supporting Associate Development

- Training, career advancement, and inclusive workplace practices.
- Diversity, equity, and inclusion strategy focused on inclusive culture.
- Cross-functional teams driving innovation and continuous improvement.
- Creative collaboration across cultures, ethnicities, genders, and disciplines.
- Expansion into new markets with sustainable practices and local engagement.

## Reinforcing Accountability

### Oversight of Sustainability Reporting

Governance structures ensure accuracy, completeness, and transparency of disclosed information.

### Public Policy Engagement

Engagement in public policy related to sustainability, water stewardship, and environmental protection.

### Commitment to Ethical Conduct

Supplier Code of Conduct and ethical business practices: anti-corruption, conflicts of interest, whistleblower protections.

### Inclusive Sourcing

Diverse suppliers included in procurement: minority-owned, women-owned, and small businesses.

### Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity programs protect information systems and data. Regular assessments and improvements to security posture.

### Data Privacy

Kohler is committed to protecting personal data in accordance with applicable privacy laws and regulations. Privacy notices and policies govern collection, use, and sharing of personal information.
