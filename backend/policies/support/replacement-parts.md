# Genuine OEM Replacement Parts & Availability

## 10-Year Parts Availability Guarantee
Kohler guarantees the commercial availability of functional replacement parts (valves, cartridges, seals, electronic modules) for a minimum of ten (10) years following the official product discontinuation date.

## Counterfeit Protection
Warranty terms apply exclusively to repairs performed using certified Genuine Kohler OEM parts. Use of unapproved third-party internal valves voids the Lifetime Limited Warranty on adjacent fixture components.
