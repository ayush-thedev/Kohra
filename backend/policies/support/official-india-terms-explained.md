# Kohler India Terms and Conditions Explained (Official)

*Source document: `0_official_policies/customer_support/Terms & Conditions Explained _ Kohler India.pdf`. Simplified guide for Kohler India customers covering service, warranty, payments, and support.*

## Scope

This document explains in simplified form the key terms and conditions for purchasing and using Kohler products in India through Kohler India Corporation Private Limited. Full terms and conditions apply as set out on the Kohler India website (kohler.co.in).

## Order Changes

Orders may be changed or cancelled by contacting Kohler Customer Care before the order is dispatched. Once the order is dispatched, changes or cancellations are no longer possible. For delivered products, the return and refund policy applies.

## Returns and Refunds

- Products may be returned within 30 days of delivery in original, unused condition with all packaging and accessories.
- Return shipping costs are borne by the customer unless the return is due to a Kohler error (wrong product shipped, defective product).
- Refunds processed within 7–10 business days after Kohler receives and inspects the returned product. Refund credited to the original payment method.
- Custom or made-to-order products are non-returnable.
- For defective products covered under warranty, Kohler will repair, replace, or refund at its discretion.

## Payments and Processing

- Accepted payment methods: credit/debit cards, net banking, UPI, and EMI options.
- All payments processed through secure, RBI-compliant payment gateways.
- Product pricing is inclusive of applicable GST unless otherwise stated.
- Installation charges, if applicable, are separate and communicated before service.

## Warranty Coverage

- Kohler warrants products against material and manufacturing defects per the applicable Kohler Product Limited Warranty.
- Warranty period starts from the date of purchase as recorded by Kohler or the authorized dealer.
- Warranty does not cover damage from misuse, neglect, accident, improper installation, use of non-Kohler parts, or unauthorized modifications.
- Warranty claims require proof of purchase (invoice or order confirmation).
- For smart and electronic products, warranty terms specific to electronic components apply.

## Customer Care and Service

- Kohler India Customer Care: toll-free number 1800-209-2657 (within India), email: cust_service@kohler.com.
- Service available Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (excluding public holidays).
- For product installation, contact Kohler Customer Care or the authorized dealer where the product was purchased.
- Installation by unauthorized personnel may void the warranty.

## Installation Services

- Professional installation available for plumbing fixtures, smart toilets, and kitchen/bath products.
- Installation charges vary by product type, location, and complexity. Charges communicated before scheduling.
- Customer must ensure access to the installation area, water supply, and electrical connections (for smart products) at the scheduled time.
- Kohler is not liable for pre-existing plumbing or structural issues encountered during installation.
- Installation by Kohler or authorized installers is recommended to maintain warranty validity.

## Escalation and Grievance Redressal

- First point of contact: Kohler Customer Care (1800-209-2657 or cust_service@kohler.com).
- If unresolved within 7 business days, escalate to the Grievance Officer via email: grievance.officer@kohler.com.
- Grievance Officer will acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 30 days.
- For further escalation: write to Kohler India Corporation Private Limited, registered office at 26A, Lajpat Nagar, New Delhi, Delhi 110024.

## Customer Responsibilities

- Provide accurate and complete information at the time of purchase and installation.
- Ensure the installation area meets Kohler's specifications (water pressure, drainage, electrical supply).
- Follow product care and maintenance instructions provided with the product.
- Report defects or issues promptly to Kohler Customer Care.

## Product Handling and Storage

- Products must be stored in dry, temperature-controlled environments before installation.
- Plumbing fixtures should not be subjected to excessive force, chemical exposure, or impact.
- Smart and electronic products must not be exposed to water beyond their IP rating.

## Limitation of Liability

- Kohler's liability is limited to the purchase price of the product.
- Kohler is not liable for indirect, consequential, or incidental damages arising from product use or installation.
- Kohler is not responsible for plumbing, electrical, or structural work performed by third parties.

## Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Disputes subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in New Delhi.
