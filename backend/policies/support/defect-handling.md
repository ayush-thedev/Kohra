# Product Defect Triage & Replacement Handling

## Defect Severity Classification
- Critical Defect (Water damage risk, electrical failure, cracked basin): Immediate priority replacement dispatched within 24 hours via express courier with RMA prepaid label.
- Major Defect (Finish discoloration, mechanical linkage jam): Replacement part or assembly dispatched within 48 to 72 hours upon photo verification.
- Minor Cosmetic Flaw: Customer offered 15% goodwill credit or touch-up restoration kit.

## Return Material Authorization (RMA) Workflow
Customer care agents generate RMA numbers within Salesforce Service Cloud. Damaged units flagged for quality engineering root-cause analysis must be routed directly to the Kohler Quality Lab in Kohler, Wisconsin.
