# Kohler Product Limited Warranty Policy

## Standard 1-Year Limited Warranty
Kohler Co. warrants its residential plumbing fixtures, faucets, and vitreous china bathroom products against material and manufacturing defects for a period of one (1) year from the verified date of purchase by the original consumer purchaser.

## Lifetime Faucet Cartridge Warranty
Kohler ceramic disc faucet valves and cartridges carry a Lifetime Limited Warranty to be leak-free and drip-free during normal residential usage for as long as the original purchaser owns their home.

## Electronic & Smart Fixtures Warranty
Kohler smart home fixtures, Konnect intelligent toilets, digital valves, and motorized shower components carry a 3-Year Limited Electronic Component Warranty covering replacement parts and factory diagnostic support.
