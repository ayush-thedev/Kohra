# Customer Support Escalation Protocols

## Multi-Tier Resolution Framework
- Tier 1 (Frontline Support): First-contact resolution for basic specs, order tracking, and warranty verification. SLA: Under 2 hours response time.
- Tier 2 (Technical Specialists): Advanced plumbing troubleshooting, rough-in schematics, Konnect WiFi pairing diagnostics. SLA: 4 hours.
- Tier 3 (Master Field Plumber & Product Engineering): Escalations involving structural plumbing leaks, commercial installations, or legal notice mentions. SLA: 1 hour immediate outreach.

## Executive Inquiries & High-Profile Escalations
Any inquiry from architectural firms, luxury hotel developers, or regulatory bodies must be simultaneously tagged to the Director of Enterprise Client Success and Corporate Legal within 30 minutes.
